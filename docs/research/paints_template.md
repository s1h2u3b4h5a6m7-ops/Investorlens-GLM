# Research Template: Paints

## Source hierarchy

1. **DRHPs** — SEBI filings (Asian Paints, Berger, Kansai Nerolac, Akzo Nobel)
   - Extract: raw material breakdown, TiO2 sourcing, decorative vs industrial mix, dealer network

2. **Annual reports** — Company IR pages
   - Extract: raw material cost as % of revenue, inventory levels, segment breakdown

3. **Credit rating rationales** — CRISIL, ICRA
   - Extract: crude oil linkage, TiO2 cost %, pricing power, market share

## Key research questions

### Raw materials

### Cost structure
- [ ] Raw material as % of revenue (target: ~55%)

- [ ] Packaging cost as % of revenue
- [ ] Freight cost as % of revenue

### Demand drivers
- [ ] Decorative vs industrial split (target: ~70/30)

### Pricing and margins

- [ ] How does the gross margin trend with crude oil cycle?

### Market structure
